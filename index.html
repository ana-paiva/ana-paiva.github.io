About

Engineering sociality into machines

For more than three decades my research has centred on one question: how do we build autonomous agents and robots that interact with humans in a fair, transparent, social and natural way?

I am a Full Professor of Artificial Intelligence in the Department of Computer Engineering at Instituto Superior Técnico (IST), University of Lisbon, and a senior researcher at INESC-ID. In 2000 I founded GAIPS, the Group on AI for People and Society, one of the internationally leading groups in social AI, which I coordinated for twenty years.

My work engineers AI systems that exhibit specific social capabilities: social perception, social adaptation, following norms, collaboration, resolving social conflicts, and verbal and non-verbal behaviour, among others. I bring these together in a framework for Artificial Social Intelligence, and apply them across intelligent learning environments, social robotics, social simulation, serious games and entertainment. Throughout, I place the human in the loop, working towards AI that is trustworthy and centred on human values: AI for people and society.

I also believe that AI is changing the way we live, work, and, for us scientists, the way we teach and conduct science. Our society will change and new policies must be developed to adapt to this new world. Between April 2024 and June 2025 I served as Secretary of State for Science in the XXIV Portuguese Government, overseeing national science and technology policy, and contributing to this world in change.

Research

Artificial Social Intelligence

There is a difference between social artificial intelligence and Artificial Social Intelligence. My work is about the latter: giving machines the intelligence to act socially, so they can perceive, adapt, and take part in the social worlds of people. A single framework of social capabilities ties the threads of my research together.

The framework

Acting socially

To interact well with people, an agent needs more than task competence. It needs to read a social situation and act within it. I study how to engineer these capabilities and how to compose them, so that agents and robots become genuine social partners rather than mere tools. The framework anchors every thread that follows.

A framework for building social intelligence

Artificial Social Intelligence

The core framework: engineering social understanding, interpersonal competencies, social decision making and social responsibility into agents, so that they can act socially. This thread relates to, and underpins, all the others.

Social capabilitiesActing sociallyHuman-centred AI

Empathy and affect

Affective computing and empathy in agents and robots: modelling emotions, recognising them, and using them in the communication between humans and machines, including long-term interaction and the role of humour in human-robot interaction.

Affective computingEmpathyHumour in HRI

Social robots, groups and teams

Human-robot interaction beyond the one-robot, one-user paradigm: robots that take part in groups and teams, hybrid societies of humans and robots, membership and team formation, trust, and inclusion.

HRIGroups and teamsHybrid societies

Prosocial AI and cooperation

Engineering pro-sociality with autonomous agents: cooperation, fairness and social dilemmas studied through multi-agent systems and evolutionary game theory, towards societies of humans and agents that are more cooperative and altruistic.

ProsocialityCooperationSocial dilemmas

AI for Learning and society

Intelligent learning environments and social good: empathic tutors, education for children with neurodevelopmental conditions, serious games, storytelling, cultural understanding and social simulation.
